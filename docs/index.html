How it works

Three taps a day. A year of insight.

No streaks to defend, no scores to chase — just an honest record of your days and a clear way to see them.

1

Log your day in seconds

Define the topics you care about — each with a scale that fits, from a simple yes/no to five levels like “low → great.” Then one scrollable screen lists them all; tap to record the day. Nothing is pre-selected, so “not logged” is never mistaken for a real value. A daily reminder nudges you at a time you choose.

2

See the year as a heatmap

Each day becomes a colored cell in a year grid; darker means higher, grey means a day you didn’t log. Seasonal rhythms and rough patches jump out at a glance — a whole year on one screen.

3

Overlay topics to spot patterns

Pick a second topic and the grid splits each day in two, side by side. Eyeball whether mood tracks sleep, or headaches follow a rough week — visual and honest, without inventing false statistical precision from thin, self-reported data.

Why Journalo

Built to respect your data and your attention.

🔒

Local-only by design

Everything lives on your device. No server, no cloud, no account to create. Your records are yours.

✈️

Fully offline

Journalo never needs the network. It works on a plane, in a tunnel, anywhere — because nothing leaves your phone.

🙈

No tracking, ever

No analytics, no ads, no third-party SDKs phoning home. There’s simply nothing collecting data about you.

🎯

Honest data

A blank day is genuinely blank — never quietly counted as zero. The app keeps “not logged” and a real low value strictly apart.

↩️

Nothing is lost

Edits append to a history rather than overwriting it, so you can change a past day without rewriting the record.

💸

One-time purchase

Buy it once. No subscription, no upsells, no in-app purchases — just the app.
