This policy covers two separate things: the Journalo app on your device, and this website, where the app is presented.

1. The Journalo app

The app processes no personal data on any external system. Everything you enter (your topics and daily values) is stored only locally on your device. There is:

  • no server and no cloud storage,
  • no user account and no sign-in,
  • no synchronisation between devices,
  • no analytics, tracking, or advertising services,
  • no network connection that transmits your content.

The app sends no data to us or to third parties. Backups are created only through the operating-system features you choose yourself (for example Apple's iCloud backup or Android's backup feature); these are subject to the respective provider's privacy terms and are outside our control. Deleting the app removes the locally stored data from your device.
